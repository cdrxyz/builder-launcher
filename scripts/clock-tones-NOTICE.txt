Android Open Source Project alarm and ringtone clips
====================================================

The files in app/src/main/res/raw/clock_*.ogg are copied from
platform/frameworks/base data/sounds (Apache License 2.0):

  clock_pulse.ogg     <- alarms/ogg/Neon.ogg
  clock_chime.ogg     <- ringtones/ogg/Callisto.ogg
  clock_bell.ogg      <- ringtones/ogg/Titania.ogg
  clock_orthodox.ogg  <- ringtones/ogg/Kuma.ogg
  clock_hum.ogg       <- ringtones/ogg/Themos.ogg

https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/
