## What it is

Builder Launcher is an Android home screen for people who want the phone to stay out of the way. Type a command, send the message, check the weather, then go back to the rest of the day.

Works on ordinary slab phones and hardware-keyboard devices such as the Unihertz Titan 2 Elite.
