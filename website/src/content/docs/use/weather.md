---
title: Weather
description: Full forecast from the home weather line — temperature, hourly, daily, and details.
---

![Full weather screen](../../../assets/screenshots/weather.png)

Tap the weather line under the clock on [home](home/), or type `weather` / `/weather`.

The screen follows the usual forecast layout: large temperature with a weather illustration to its right, feels / precip / wind, next 24 hours with condition icons, seven days with an icon and a short note (precip chance and amount, or fog / storms / high UV), then humidity, dew point, UV, pressure, visibility, cloud cover, sunrise / sunset, wind, gusts, and air quality.

Data comes from Open-Meteo (plus Open-Meteo air quality). Units follow the metric / imperial setting. Set the city in [settings](../configure/weather/). `<` returns home.
