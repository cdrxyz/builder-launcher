title: Usage
description: Screen time on the phone. Productive vs distracting, last 7 days, most-used apps.
---

![Usage with a 7-day chart and most-used apps](../../../assets/screenshots/usage.png)

Type `usage` or `/usage`, tap the descending bar chart at the top left of [home](home/), swipe left from home, or press D-pad left.

Android keeps the totals. Builder Launcher does not upload them. Grant **Usage access** the first time (also in [settings](../../configure/settings/)).

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Today / 7 days | Switch the total, breakdown, and most-used list. The chart is always the last 7 days. |
| Total | Time in the foreground for the selected period. |
| vs yesterday | Shown for **today**. How much more or less than the previous day. |
| Pickups | Screen-on events for today, or the week when 7 days is selected. |
| Chart | Stacked bars: productive (accent), distracting (red), other (dim). |
| Breakdown | Share of productive / distracting / other. |
| Most used | Top apps. Tap a row to cycle **other → productive → distracting**. Your choice sticks on the device. |

YouTube, Instagram, and similar apps start as distracting. Termux, Slack, Gmail, calendars, and maps start as productive. Everything else starts as other, including this launcher and browsers.

Swipe right, `<`, or Back returns home.
