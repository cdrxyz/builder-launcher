---
title: Videos
description: Save a YouTube link, play the picture in Videos, and the audio from Podcasts.
---

Type `videos` or `/videos`.

The bar takes a YouTube link. Enter saves the title and channel. It does not store the stream. Play asks YouTube again, because those addresses expire.

In Videos, play shows the picture. In Podcasts, Saved Videos sits with Saved Articles. The now playing bar there plays the audio only, and a tap opens the video.

A private or unavailable video stays unplayed and says why.
