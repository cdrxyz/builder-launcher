---
title: Weather
description: City autocomplete, no GPS required. Metric or imperial on the home clock.
---

![Weather location and units in settings](../../../assets/screenshots/settings.png)

1. Settings → **Weather location**.
2. Type a city (placeholder is New York). Pick a match from geocoding suggestions.
3. Home shows a condition icon over the temperature between the clock and the ticker. Tap that mark for the [full forecast](../use/weather/).

![Weather mark between the clock and ticker on home](../../../assets/screenshots/home.png)

- No GPS is required when a city is set. If you clear the city, the launcher may use a last-known GPS point when the OS has already granted location — it does not request a live fix. The documented path is still "type a city".
- **Weather units:** `metric` (Celsius) or `imperial` (Fahrenheit). Default metric.
- Forecast refreshes about every 15 minutes from Open-Meteo.

Clear the field to stop showing weather.
